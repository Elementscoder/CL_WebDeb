Order of operations:

The order of operation is important to know in math too, if you didn't do in the right order it couldn't always be right. For remembering the order of operation with math you need to know PEMDAS. PEMDAS is the order your supposed to do math, it stand for Parentheses, Exponents, Multiplication, Division, Addition, and Subtraction. Once you have that then you need to remember that multiplication and division are in the same line of order, meaning you can do either or first and it won't matter, addition and subtraction have the same line of order too. But don't think multiplication and addition are in the same line of order because it will matter if you do addition first, before multiplication. Also when only one line of order in the equation then you do it like reading, you do the math from left to right.

PEMDAS:

  1. Parentheses
  2. Exponents
  3. Multiply
  4. Divide
  5. Add
  6. Subtract
