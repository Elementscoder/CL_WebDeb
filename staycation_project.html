St. George

About St. George:

St. George is a fun place to come to Utah for a least once. In St. George you can live the condo life and hopefully live by a pool that you can go into anytime for free if you have a membership card for the pool. You could also go golfing near by the condos and have fun with your family and/or friends.

Images from Wikimdeia Commons

Four reasons you should go:

  1. Fun family and friend time.
  2. You can swim in pools anytime.
  3. You can golf near the place your staying.
  4. Get to live the condo life.

Personal Experience:

For me going to St. George was my only vacation every other year, so it was pretty fun for me. Also it was cool because I had never had experienced having a cable telivision, for me that was a fun experience, but it got kind of boring as the years passed. When I went there with my family, we usually go swimming and have fun at the pool most of the day. We also got to have more family time than at home and got to play more board games together, which is always a fun time, since we barely do it. A fun thing we also do is play pool, which is fun for me to get experience with it and get better at playing it.
